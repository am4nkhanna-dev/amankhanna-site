About Mr Khanna

Consultant ENT Surgeon with a specialist interest in rhinology and facial plastics.

Practice

Mr Aman Khanna has been a Consultant ENT Surgeon at University Hospitals Birmingham NHS Foundation Trust since October 2018. His practice centres on the nose and sinuses: nasal blockage, sinus disease, nasal allergy, endoscopic sinus surgery, and septorhinoplasty for both function and appearance. He also assesses and operates for snoring and sleep apnoea, and sees patients with general ear, nose and throat problems.

In the NHS he runs two joint clinics. At Heartlands Hospital he works alongside respiratory physicians for patients whose nose and sinus disease is linked to asthma and other chest conditions. At the Queen Elizabeth Hospital Birmingham he works with the haematology team to care for people with bleeding disorders who have recurrent nosebleeds.

He sees private patients at The Priory Hospital and The Harborne Hospital in Birmingham, and at The Droitwich Spa Hospital.

Training

Mr Khanna qualified in medicine at the University of Leicester in 2005, having also gained a BSc with honours for research on visual perception.

He trained in ENT surgery across the East Midlands, at Leicester Royal Infirmary, Royal Derby Hospital and Queen's Medical Centre in Nottingham, and was awarded the FRCS (ORL-HNS) in 2016.

He then spent a year as the rhinology fellow at Charing Cross Hospital in London, a Royal College of Surgeons accredited post in rhinology and skull base sinus surgery, including joint clinics with the respiratory allergy team at the Royal Brompton Hospital.

Teaching

Mr Khanna has been the ENT Undergraduate Year 4 Curriculum Lead at the University of Birmingham since 2019, and a Clinical Simulation Tutor at University Hospitals Birmingham since 2023.

He is on the faculty of the regional sinus surgery and rhinoplasty dissection courses for surgeons in training, and has taught on FRCS revision courses and national ENT masterclasses.

He is a director of ORY Academy, a community interest company that runs meetings and courses for clinicians, including the Medical Rhinology Meeting in Birmingham.

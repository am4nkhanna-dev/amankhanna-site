Specialist care for the nose, sinuses and face

Mr Aman Khanna is a Consultant ENT Surgeon in Birmingham. He specialises in rhinology and facial plastic surgery, including sinus surgery, nasal allergy and rhinoplasty, and sees patients with all ear, nose and throat problems.

What Mr Khanna treats

Most patients come with a blocked nose, sinus trouble, allergy, or a nose they would like to breathe through or look at more comfortably. He also treats snoring and runs a full general ENT practice.

Nose and sinuses

Rhinology, from first assessment to surgery

  • Blocked nose and deviated septum
  • Sinusitis and endoscopic sinus surgery
  • Nasal polyps and loss of smell
  • Nosebleeds

Nasal allergy

Hay fever, dust mite, pet and mould allergy

  • Allergy testing
  • Sprays, tablets and rinses
  • Desensitisation treatment
  • Surgery for a blocked nose

Rhinoplasty and facial plastics

Surgery that considers how the nose works as well as how it looks

  • Septorhinoplasty
  • Cosmetic rhinoplasty
  • Bent or injured nose
  • Scars and facial skin lesions

Snoring, ear and throat

Snoring and sleep apnoea, and general ENT care

  • Snoring and sleep apnoea
  • Hearing loss, ear wax and tinnitus
  • Blocked ears and dizziness
  • Sore throat and voice problems

About Mr Khanna

Mr Khanna has been a Consultant ENT Surgeon at University Hospitals Birmingham since 2018. Before that he completed a Royal College of Surgeons accredited fellowship in rhinology and sinus surgery at Charing Cross Hospital in London.

He leads ENT teaching for fourth-year medical students at the University of Birmingham and trains surgeons on sinus surgery and rhinoplasty courses across the Midlands.

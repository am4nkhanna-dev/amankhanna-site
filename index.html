Mr Aman Khanna is a Consultant ENT Surgeon in Birmingham. He specialises in rhinology and facial plastic surgery, including sinus surgery, nasal allergy and rhinoplasty, and sees patients with all ear, nose and throat problems.

What Mr Khanna treats

About Mr Khanna

Mr Khanna has been a Consultant ENT Surgeon at University Hospitals Birmingham since 2018. Before that he completed a Royal College of Surgeons accredited fellowship in rhinology and sinus surgery at Charing Cross Hospital in London.

He leads ENT teaching for fourth-year medical students at the University of Birmingham and trains surgeons on sinus surgery and rhinoplasty courses across the Midlands.

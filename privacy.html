Privacy notice

How information you send through this website is used.

What is collected

This website does not ask you to create an account. The only personal information it collects is what you choose to enter in the appointment request form: your name, contact details, how the appointment will be paid for, your preferred clinic, and anything you write about the reason for your visit.

How it is used

Your details are used only to arrange your appointment. They are seen by Mr Khanna and his practice manager, Farah Baig, and are passed to the hospital or clinic where you choose to be seen so that it can register you.

Services this website relies on

The appointment form is provided by Tally, which stores submissions on Mr Khanna's behalf. Patient reviews are displayed by Doctify, and fonts are supplied by Google Fonts. These services may record technical information such as your IP address when their content loads.

Cookies

This website sets only the cookies and similar storage it needs to work, plus a note of your cookie choice on your own device. If you choose Accept, the site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, pages viewed, countries and device types, without tracking you across other websites; it loads only after you choose Accept. Declining does not affect how you use the site. Content supplied by Doctify, Tally and Google Fonts may set its own cookies when it loads. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you by contacting Farah Baig at farahbaig@protonmail.com. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Medical records

Records of your consultation and treatment are kept by the hospital or clinic where you are seen, under its own privacy policy.

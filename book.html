Fees and booking

Self-pay and insured patients are both welcome. You can book directly with the hospital, online or through its call centre. If you would prefer, Farah Baig, Mr Khanna's practice manager, can arrange your appointment for you.

Self-pay fees

£350New consultation
£300Follow-up consultation

All-inclusive fee for standard outpatient procedures

  • Mr Khanna does not charge any other fee for standard outpatient procedures at your consultation, such as a nasal endoscopy (a camera examination of the nose and/or throat).
  • Minor operations and other specialised procedures may cost more, and these are always discussed with you beforehand. If surgery is recommended, the costs are set out before you decide.
  • Any charges made by the hospital, such as its facility fee or tests and investigations it carries out, are billed separately by the hospital. Farah can tell you what to expect at each site before you book.

What to expect at your first visit

Mr Khanna will ask about your symptoms and general health, then examine your nose, ears and throat. Many nose and throat problems are looked at with a thin camera, which is quick and done in the clinic.

Please bring:

  • any referral letter you have
  • a list of your medicines and allergies
  • previous scan reports or hospital letters
  • your insurance details and authorisation number, if you are insured

Before you leave, he will explain what he has found and what the options are, so you can decide what to do next.

Do I need a referral letter?

A letter from your GP is usually not needed for a self-pay private appointment. If you have private medical insurance, your insurer will often ask for one before it agrees to pay, so it is worth checking with them first.

A GP letter is also helpful because it gives Mr Khanna your medical history. If you have one, please bring it.
